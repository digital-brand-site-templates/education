Frequently Asked Questions

Straight answers to what applicants and parents ask us most.

Can I give a guest lecture?

Guest lectures are arranged through the school that matches your subject. Email demo@info.com with a short outline and a date range, and the head of school will reply within two weeks. Most guest talks run on Wednesday afternoons.

Can I study abroad for a term?

Several courses include an exchange term with one of our twelve partner universities. Speak to the international office in your first year: places are allocated in February for the following autumn, and travel grants are available.

Can I take a break between courses?

On part-time and short courses, yes. You can pause between modules and pick up again within two years without re-applying. Full-time degrees follow a fixed timetable, but your tutor can help if something comes up.

Can I pay tuition in instalments?

Yes. Every plan can be paid in two instalments, in September and February, at no extra cost. Monthly payments are possible on the All-access Year; ask the finance office to set it up.

How many courses can I take at once?

As many as your timetable allows. Most students take four to six courses a term. Part-time and full-time tuition is priced by the credits you take each year; the All-access Year has no cap.

Still not sure? Our admissions team answers calls on weekdays from 09:00 to 17:00.
