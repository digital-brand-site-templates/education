Creative Web Design

Eight weeks, two evenings a week, one real website for a real client at the end of it.

About this course

Creative Web Design is for people who want to make websites that look good and work properly, whether you are starting from nothing or have tinkered with a page builder before. You will learn how designers think about layout, type and colour, then build those ideas in HTML and CSS yourself.

The last three weeks are spent on a live brief from a local business or charity. You present your site to them in the final session, and many students leave with their first portfolio piece already online.

What you will learn

  • Plan a site from a brief: pages, content and the people who will use it
  • Lay out pages with CSS grid and flexbox that work on phones and desktops
  • Choose type and colour that read well and meet accessibility guidelines
  • Prepare images so pages stay fast
  • Put a finished site online and hand it over to a client

Course outline

Weeks 1–2How the web works, and your first page

Browsers, servers and files. Writing clean HTML, linking pages and adding your first styles.

Weeks 3–4Layout that adapts to any screen

Grid, flexbox and media queries, practised on three small layouts you can reuse later.

Week 5Type, colour and accessible design

Pairing fonts, building a palette and checking contrast, with a short design critique each session.

Weeks 6–8The client project

Meet your client, design and build their site in a small team, then present it in the final session.

Who it is for

No experience is needed, only a laptop and about four hours a week outside class. If you have built simple pages already, Sara will give you a harder brief.
