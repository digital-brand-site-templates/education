Admissions

Applying takes about twenty minutes. We reply within ten working days, with an interview date if your course has one.

How to apply

  • Choose your course

    Browse the course pages, or come to an open day and sit in on a taster lecture first.

  • Send the form below

    Your details, your course, your previous study and a short personal statement.

  • Talk to us

    Some courses invite you to a twenty-minute online interview. It is a conversation, not a test.

  • Get your answer

    Within ten working days of applying, with any scholarship decision included in the same letter.

Key dates

Applications for most courses stay open until the course is full, but scholarship applications close earlier.
