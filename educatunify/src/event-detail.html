November Open Day

Saturday 7 November, 10:00 to 15:00. Tours, taster lectures and a chance to ask current students anything.

What happens on the day

The open day is the easiest way to find out whether Educatunify feels right. Every school runs a short talk and a taster lecture, current students lead tours every half hour, and the admissions, finance and accommodation teams are on hand all day.

You do not need to stay for everything. Most visitors come for two or three hours, pick the talks for the subjects they are interested in and finish with lunch in the Commons.

  • 11:00

  • 12:30

  • 13:30

  • 15:00

Getting here

Buses 14 and 22 stop at the main gate, and Richmond Station is a nine-minute walk. Free visitor parking is at the West Gate; arrive by 10:15 for the welcome talk.
